8-Lesson Curriculum

Each lesson combines theory with hands-on building. By the end, you'll have a deployed product with real users.

From zero to deployed startup in 8 weeks.

1

Idea → Product: Thinking Like a Founder

Learn to identify real problems and validate solutions. Understand the difference between ideas and viable products.

Deliverable:

Your product idea + target users clearly defined

2

Phases, MVPs & Breaking Your Product Down

Master the art of scoping. Learn to break big ideas into buildable phases and define your Minimum Viable Product.

Deliverable:

3 Product Phases mapped out (Core, Enhanced, Future)

3

HTML Foundations: Build Your First Page

Start coding! Learn HTML structure and build the foundation of your product's homepage.

Deliverable:

Homepage structure (HTML only) with your product's core message

4

CSS Styling: Make It Look Good

Transform your HTML into a polished interface. Learn CSS fundamentals and design principles.

Deliverable:

Fully styled homepage that looks professional

5

JavaScript Basics: Make It Interactive

Add functionality to your product. Learn JavaScript fundamentals and make your pages interactive.

Deliverable:

Interactive feature working on your site (button clicks, forms, or dynamic content)

6

GitHub: Version Control & Collaboration

Learn professional development workflows. Master Git and GitHub for code management and collaboration.

Deliverable:

Project on GitHub with at least 5 commits showing your progress

7

Deployment: Ship to Real Users

Launch day! Deploy your product to the internet using Netlify or GitHub Pages. Share your live URL with the world.

Deliverable:

Live product URL that anyone can access

8

Next Steps: Growth & Iteration

Learn to gather feedback, measure usage, and plan your next features. Understand how real products evolve.

Deliverable:

Phase 2 plan + one user testing session completed
