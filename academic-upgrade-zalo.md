## The story behind it (for the salesperson, not for the parent)

Two kinds of parents kept asking for the same thing from different directions:

1. Parents who take their child's education seriously and want to see the actual work,
   week by week — not a summary at the end.
2. Parents preparing a study-abroad application, who need supporting material to show.

The upgrade is one answer to both: the work gets graded and written down, so the parent
can follow it now **and** has something concrete to show later.

Use whichever half fits the parent in front of you. Don't recite both.

## EN — first message

Academic Upgrade: +2.000.000₫ per 24-lesson program (about 83.000₫ a lesson).

Same teachers, same lessons. What changes:
• Homework becomes required, graded, saved to your child's account
• A recording of every lesson, sent to you
• An extended report every 8 lessons — skills learned, grades, what comes next
• A teacher-polished portfolio, ready to submit

It isn't better teaching — it's being able to check progress instead of trusting it.

---

## Answering "what do I actually get after each lesson?"

The lesson recording, and the graded homework with the teacher's comment. Same week —
not at the end of the program.

---

## Do not promise

- **Report cards, academic transcripts, GPA, or recommendation letters.** Not part of
  the upgrade. If a parent asks, say we give the extended report every 8 lessons and
  the graded portfolio — and take their request back to Gleb.
- Grades backdated to lessons before the upgrade started.
- Recordings in a Shared Learning class unless **every** family in that class has
  agreed to be recorded. Otherwise recordings are private-only for that class.
