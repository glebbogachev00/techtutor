## What this actually is

TechTutor Academy teaches coding, AI, game development and 3D design to children and teens,
ages 7 to 17. Live online classes over Zoom, bilingual English and Vietnamese. Students in
Vietnam, the USA, the UK and elsewhere.

**TechBash** is the product and the portal, and it is positioned as an **incubator for young
builders — not a coding course.** The point is kids who leave as problem-solvers and makers
with a foundation to go build their own thing.

Core ethos: **build something real people actually use.** Ventures come from real problems in
a kid's own world — siblings, school, chores, games. Success is "shipped something a real
person actually used," never "missions completed."

The founder loop is the spine: find a real problem, build an MVP, get a real user, iterate,
pitch. Tasted early and small, scaling to a demo-day capstone.

Gleb is a **solopreneur** and dislikes YC / venture-startup framing. Never write about
TechTutor in startup-accelerator language. **Manthan** is his partner, not merely staff.

## Structure

- Programs are **24-lesson bundles**. Every student completes **6 real projects** plus a portfolio.
- **90-minute classes with two or more students; 60 minutes if solo.** 2 to 3 times per week.
- Instructors hold Bachelor's degrees in CS, IT or Software Engineering. They write software
  for a living.
- Commitments are portable across all pathways — a paid session is never lost.

Scratch Game Coder · GDevelop Game Designer · Roblox World Creator · AI & Programming Quest ·
Generative AI Magic · Python Fundamentals · Website Creation · App Development · Startup
Foundations · 3D Designer. All 24 sessions each.

Tools by name: Scratch, GDevelop, Roblox with Lua, Python, Thunkable, Blender.

## The two formats

**Private Coaching (1 on 1)** — 15,000,000₫ / about $608 per 24-lesson program.

**Shared Learning (group of 2 to 4)** — 8,400,000₫ / about $346 per program. The most popular
option. It **starts private until classmates join**, which is why it runs 60 minutes solo and
90 minutes once there are two or more.

Important nuance for honest copy: **in practice everything is currently 1-on-1 even when it is
labelled group.** Do not claim a kid will have classmates.

## The two journeys

**Explorer** — the curriculum and the teachers.

**Academy** — +2,000,000₫ / about $80. Adds graded homework, tracked skills, a showcase
portfolio, a transcript and a recommendation letter. Built for parents who want a record.

The site's own framing of the difference is worth reusing, because it is honest and it is
exactly our voice:

> Same curriculum, same teachers. Academy gives you more accountability and a record you can
> keep — not better teaching.

## Price discipline

Quote at most **one** price per post, the one the story earned.

- Story about a parent who wanted visibility → the Academy upgrade.
- Story about a kid getting individual attention → Private Coaching.
- Story about cost or commitment → Shared Learning.
- Most stories → no price at all. The free trial is usually the better ask.

A post listing the format price, the upgrade, the lesson count and the duration is a rate
card, not an ad.
