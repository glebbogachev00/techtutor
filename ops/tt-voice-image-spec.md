# TechTutor Content System — Voice + Image Spec

## Brand colors (Tech Bash / Tech Tutor)
- **Anchor:** deep navy / cosmic purple backgrounds
- **Accent 1:** Mochi orange `#FFA500`
- **Accent 2:** Python blue `#3776AB`
- **Accent 3:** Python yellow `#FFD43B` (sparingly)
- **Rule:** bold black outlines, flat shapes, no gradients.
- **Contrast is not optional.** Soft off-white on pale blue disappears into the
  feed background. Use a saturated block or a near-black ground so the image has
  an edge against white UI.
- **Never 'undraw style', never 'calm and uncluttered'.** Those two phrases produce
  the generic startup illustration that nobody looks at. Real photos and real
  screenshots of student work beat any illustration for stopping a scroll.
- **Never:** teal/green/orange/gold as UI accents. Those belong to Mochi or Python-brand moments only.

## Platform voices

### IG — Mochi, nudging peer
- One beat per post.
- Funny or impressive, never both. Pick one.
- Mochi reacts; don’t make Mochi lecture.
- Caption: 1–2 sentences max. Hashtags only if they earn reach.
- Image/video: Mochi + kid work, or kid work only. 9:16.

### FB — Calm advisor
- One signal per post: capacity, result, or flexibility.
- Specific parent moment beats general benefit.
- Honest capacity language: “we’re almost booked for this month” / “a few trial spots left” — true, not urgent.
- CTA: one link or one DM ask. No “click here.”
- Image: one arresting thing, high contrast, legible at thumbnail size. 4:5.

### Reddit — Peer educator
- Teach one thing. Wrong turns and counter-examples are the good stuff.
- No Mochi. No brand voice. Just a person who knows the territory.
- Structure: problem → what we tried → the insight → link at end.
- Title: specific. “How a 10-year-old debugged a Scratch collision bug” beats “Teaching kids to code.”

## Image prompt templates

```
IG:
Clean [photo of kid hands on laptop showing their built project | flat illustration | screenshot frame],
Mochi reacting nearby with [expression], deep-blue (#193B92) and Mochi orange (#FFA500) accents only.
Bold black outlines, flat shapes, no gradients, no text, no logos, no watermark. 9:16 portrait.

FB:
Clean flat illustration, deep-blue (#193B92) monochrome accents on soft off-white (#F4F6FB):
[specific calm moment — calendar, open door, kid at laptop from behind]. Hard contrast, bold flat shapes, no gradients, no text. 4:5 portrait.

Reddit:
Clean minimal diagram or flat illustration, deep-blue (#193B92) and white:
[simple concept flow or tool mockup — no text]. Bold outlines, flat shapes, no logos. 4:5 portrait.
```

## Hard constraints (all platforms)
- No em-dashes in prose.
- No emoji in captions.
- No bold/markdown in plain captions.
- No hype words: unlock, empower, revolutionize, cutting-edge, effortless, world-class.
- No marketing triads: “faster, smarter, better.”
- No “limited spots only!” → honest capacity language only.
- Vietnamese captions must read natural, not stiff translation.

## Mochi expressions bank
- Eye-roll at a bad variable name
- Thumbs-up from behind the laptop
- Holding a sign that says “almost”
- Facepalming at a missing semicolon
- Cheering with both arms up

Pick one per post. Don’t over-direct — the illustrator reads the caption, not the prompt dictionary.
