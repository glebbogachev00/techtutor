It was their first GDevelop lesson.

The task was a simple platformer level. Grab a chicken. Bring it back to a character. Get a key. Open a door. Move to the next level.

The brother was typing the project name. He said it out loud while he typed.

“Harry Bum Cheeks.”

I laughed. The whole class laughed.

He looked up and said, “Yep.”

Then he turned back to the computer and kept building.

By the end of the session they had a working level. The chicken moved. The character gave the key. The door opened. The next level loaded.

The name stayed. The project worked.

That is usually how it goes.
