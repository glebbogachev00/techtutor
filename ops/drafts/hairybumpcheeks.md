Midway through their first GDevelop lesson, the brother typed `hairybumpcheeks` as the project name and hit enter.

I said, “You sure about that?”

His sister said, “It’s ours now.”

Technically she wasn’t wrong. The rest of the class spent 90 seconds laughing. Then they spent the remaining 40 minutes actually building the thing. The name survived. The project shipped.

What I keep noticing: the kids who ship aren’t the ones with the most polished idea. They’re the ones with a ridiculous name, a sibling backup, and enough momentum to keep going.

If your kids have bad project names but are still building — we’ll take that every time.
