Good to know

What if we don't use all the sessions?

No problem — the sessions are there when you want them, not a schedule you have to keep. Even if your child only books one, they still have the whole platform to learn from for the full period. The sessions are help on call, not the only thing you're paying for.

Do the sessions expire?

You have roughly three months to use all eight — plenty of slack to skip a week here and there. Platform access runs for that same window.

What happens when the sessions run out?

Grab another block whenever you like. Or, if your child wants steadier, guided learning, move up to a full program — we'll help you pick the right pathway.

Is this teacher-led like your programs?

Not quite — and that's the point. Programs are led by a teacher through a set curriculum. Bash Sessions are led by your child, with a mentor to guide and unblock. Lighter, more flexible, lower commitment.
