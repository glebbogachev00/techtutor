Same curriculum, same teachers. The difference is simple: Academy gives you a clear record of your child's progress — graded homework, tracked skills, and a report you can keep.

Explorer

The complete TechTutor learning experience — creative, hands-on and paced to your child.

Best for: families who want their child to build real projects and enjoy learning to code.

  • ✓ Full TechTutor curriculum

+2,000,000 ₫ / program

Academy

Everything in Explorer, with homework held accountable and every result recorded — so you always know how your child is doing.

Best for: families who want measurable progress and a record for school or university applications.

  • ➕ Required homework — reviewed, graded & saved to the student account
  • ➕ Personal learning roadmap with clear goals & milestones

  • ➕ Showcase portfolio — teacher-polished, ready to share with schools
  • ➕ Report card with grades for skills & projects

  • ➕ Optional parent–teacher meeting each term

Both journeys have the same teachers and the same care. Academy simply gives you more accountability and a record you can keep — not better teaching.
