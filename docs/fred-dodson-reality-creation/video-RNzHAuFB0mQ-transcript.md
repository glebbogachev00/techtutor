## Key ideas (working notes for you)
- **Two methods:** action-path vs allowance-path. Action is valid, but it can become grim if it’s the only path known.
- **Desire is separation:** want/desire/Yearning affirms lack energetically — “I don’t have it.”
- **You already chose before you attained:** most results came from intention/choice, not wanting.
- **Ice cream analogy:** you don’t stand there desiring the flavor. You choose it.
- **Body position of desire:** chest, stomach, or forehead pressure.
- **Release technique:** breathe through desire without resisting, changing, or transforming it. Let it run out. May take 3–20 minutes. May return later — release again.
- **Pain behind desire:** check whether emotional pain is hiding behind desire; breathe through that too.
- **Post-release add-on:** “How would I feel if I already had it?” — body learns the new state.
- **Result of release:** goals matter less; they arrive more easily; fewer/different actions are needed; less burnout.
- **Non-attachment outcome:** even huge achievement doesn’t fulfill you if lack remains inside.
- **Identity framing:** Creator, not slave; rubber band, not overstretch.
