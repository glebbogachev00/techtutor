Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you teach? ▼

We teach students aged 6-16. Our programs are designed for different skill levels: Scratch (ages 6-10), Roblox and GDevelop (ages 8-15), 3D Design and AI & Programming (ages 10-16), and App Development (ages 12-16).

Do students need prior coding experience? ▼

No prior experience is needed! We have beginner-friendly programs like Scratch and GDevelop that start from the basics. For students with some experience, we offer more advanced programs like App Development and AI & Programming.

How long is each lesson? ▼

Each lesson is 60-90 minutes of focused, hands-on learning. One course consists of 8 lessons, which students typically complete over 8 weeks with one lesson per week.

Are lessons live or pre-recorded? ▼

All our lessons are live and interactive via video call. Students work directly with their instructor in real-time, getting immediate feedback and personalized guidance.

What equipment does my child need? ▼

Students need a computer (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook) with a stable internet connection, webcam, and microphone. All software and tools are free and we'll help you get set up during the first lesson.

Can I get a refund if my child doesn't like the program? ▼

We offer a free trial lesson so families can try before committing. If you're not satisfied after the first paid lesson, we'll provide a full refund, no questions asked.

How do I schedule lessons? ▼

After enrollment, we'll work with you to find a regular weekly time slot that fits your schedule. Lessons can be rescheduled with 24 hours notice if needed.

What if my child misses a lesson? ▼

If you notify us at least 24 hours in advance, we can reschedule the lesson at no charge. Lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours notice will be forfeited.

Do you teach in English or Vietnamese? ▼

We offer bilingual instruction in both English and Vietnamese. Our instructors can teach in either language or switch between both based on the student's preference and comfort level.

How do I get started? ▼

Simply book a free trial lesson! We'll assess your child's interests and skill level, recommend the best program, and let them experience a full lesson before you commit to any payment.
