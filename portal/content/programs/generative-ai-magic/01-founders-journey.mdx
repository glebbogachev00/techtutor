## What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that **creates** — it generates text, images, audio, video, code, and entire interactive experiences.

You've probably already used it without realising:
- 🎵 **Spotify** recommending songs you'll like
- 🎬 **Netflix** suggesting your next show
- 💬 **ChatGPT** answering questions
- 🖼️ **Midjourney** turning words into images

But here's what makes this moment special: **you can now use these tools to build your own creations.**

## How It Works (The Simple Version)

Imagine teaching a dog a trick by showing it the right action thousands of times.

Generative AI learned by processing billions of examples — every book ever written, millions of images, hours of audio. It found patterns. Now when you give it a prompt, it predicts what should come next based on everything it learned.

That's it. Patterns, at massive scale.

<Callout type="tip">
**The key insight:** AI doesn't "know" things the way you do. It predicts. Your job as a Founder is to learn how to guide those predictions toward something remarkable.
</Callout>
