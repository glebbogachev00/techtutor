## What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is software that **creates** — it generates images, videos, code, music, text, and 3D assets from simple instructions you write.

You already know some of these tools. But in this course, you're not just using them. You're **building with them.**

Here's everything you're about to have in your toolkit:

## 🎬 AI Video Platforms

Your first project is an **AI-generated trailer** — a real cinematic short that showcases what you can do. These are the platforms you'll work with:

**Runway ML** — The industry standard for AI video. Type a description, upload an image, and Runway generates a clip. Used by professional filmmakers.

**Kling AI** — Exceptional at realistic motion and character movement. Great for dramatic scenes.

**Luma Dream Machine** — Specialises in 3D-style video and camera movement. Creates stunning cinematic shots.

**Suno / Udio** — These generate music from text. Describe a mood and tempo, and you get a real soundtrack in seconds.

<Callout type="tip">
**The secret to great AI video:** It's 80% prompting, 20% tool. The same Runway prompt written badly produces something forgettable. Written well, it produces something stunning. That's why prompting is the first skill we master.
</Callout>

## 🕹️ 2D Game Platforms

Project 3 is a **2D game** — and you'll build it faster than you think.

**GDevelop** — A powerful game engine that uses events instead of code. You can make a full platformer, shooter, or puzzle game without writing a single line. AI helps you design levels, write dialogue, and generate sprites.

**Construct 3** — Browser-based, professional-grade. Used by indie game studios. AI generates the artwork and sound effects while you focus on game design.

**What AI adds:** Instead of spending hours drawing sprites and backgrounds, you prompt an image AI (like Midjourney) to generate your assets. Your game looks polished in hours, not weeks.

<Reveal label="What makes a 2D game 'good'?">
The best 2D games have three things: **a clear goal** (what's the player trying to do?), **a core loop** (one action the player repeats and gets better at), and **escalating challenge** (it gets harder as you progress). Graphics matter less than you think. Super Mario Bros was 16×16 pixel sprites. The game design is what made it legendary.
</Reveal>

## 🎮 3D Game Platforms

Project 4 pushes into three dimensions.

**Three.js** — A JavaScript library that runs 3D graphics right in the browser. No download required for players. AI (specifically Claude or ChatGPT) writes the Three.js code for you — you focus on the experience design.

**Roblox Studio** — Build entire 3D worlds. 80 million people log into Roblox every day. Building on Roblox means a built-in audience.

**Spline** — The fastest way to create interactive 3D objects and scenes for the web. No code required, and AI generates assets for you.

<Callout type="tip">
**3D tip:** The jump from 2D to 3D feels intimidating but it's mostly a mindset shift. Everything is just X, Y, and Z. Once that clicks, the rest follows.
</Callout>

## 🌐 Website & App Platforms

Projects 2 and 5 are web-based — a GenAI web app and an educational website.

**Cursor** — A code editor with AI built in. You describe what you want and it writes the code. Then you describe a change and it updates the code. You're the architect; AI is the builder.

**v0 by Vercel** — Generate full UI components from a text description. Describe a button, a dashboard, a homepage — v0 produces working React code. Instant.

**Bolt.new** — Full-stack apps from a single prompt. Describe your app idea, and Bolt scaffolds the entire project: database, backend, frontend, all connected.

**What you actually learn:** These tools don't remove the need to understand code. They make you **10× faster**. The students who understand what's happening behind the scenes get the best results.

## 💬 The Skill That Runs Through Everything: Prompting

Every single platform above — video, games, websites — responds to **prompts**. Your ability to write a great prompt determines the quality of everything you create.

This isn't a side skill. It's the core skill of this course.

### What Makes a Prompt Great?

A great prompt has four things. Remember **R.C.T.F.**:

**R — Role.** Tell the AI who it should be.
> *"You are a professional concept artist who works on Hollywood sci-fi films..."*

**C — Context.** Give it the background it needs.
> *"...I'm creating a 60-second AI trailer for a school project about young people using AI..."*

**T — Task.** Be specific about what you want.
> *"...Describe three dramatic visual scenes for this trailer, each in one sentence..."*

**F — Format.** Tell it how to structure the output.
> *"...Number each scene and use vivid, cinematic language."*

### Bad Prompt vs. Great Prompt

| | Bad | Great |
|---|---|---|
| **Video prompt** | "make a cool video" | "Cinematic drone shot flying low over a neon-lit Tokyo street at 2am, rain on the pavement reflecting purple and blue lights, slow motion, film grain" |
| **Image prompt** | "a robot" | "A friendly humanoid robot with glowing amber eyes, sitting at a desk with headphones on, studying from a holographic textbook, warm studio lighting, detailed illustration style" |
| **Code prompt** | "make a game" | "Write a simple JavaScript game where a spaceship moves left and right with arrow keys and fires bullets with spacebar. Bullets destroy randomly spawning asteroids. Show score in top left." |

<Callout type="warning">
**The biggest mistake beginners make:** They accept the first output. Real creators iterate. Send 10 follow-up prompts. Each one gets you closer to what you actually want.
</Callout>

## Prompting In Action

Let's try it right now. Here's a basic prompt for your AI Trailer:

> "Create a trailer script about AI."

That's terrible. Let's build a better version using R.C.T.F.:

<Reveal label="See the R.C.T.F. version">
**Role:** You are a Hollywood trailer voiceover writer known for dramatic, inspirational scripts.

**Context:** I'm a 13-year-old creator making a 60-second short film called "The Founders" — about young people using generative AI to build the future.

**Task:** Write a 60-second voiceover script with short, punchy sentences. Build tension in the first 30 seconds. End with an inspiring call to action.

**Format:** Use dramatic pauses marked as [PAUSE]. Write stage directions in brackets. End with the tagline: "The future doesn't wait. Neither should you."
</Reveal>

The gap between those two prompts is the gap between an average result and something you'd actually be proud to share.
