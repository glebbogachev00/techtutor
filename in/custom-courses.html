How Custom Courses Work

1

Free Discovery Call (30 min)

We discuss your child's project idea, current skills, and learning goals. No commitment required — just an honest conversation about what's possible.

2

Custom Curriculum Design (2-3 days)

Our instructors create a detailed 12-lesson plan with clear milestones, tool requirements, and learning outcomes. You'll receive a document outlining:

  • • Phase-by-phase curriculum breakdown
  • • Required tools and software (with costs, if any)
  • • Concrete learning outcomes for each phase
  • • Final project deliverables
3

Weekly Live Lessons (12 sessions)

Your child works directly with their instructor through interactive video lessons. Each session builds toward completing their final project, with homework assignments for practice between classes.

4

Weekly Progress Reports

After each lesson, you receive detailed feedback on what your child learned, built, and what's coming next. Full transparency on progress.

5

Final Project Showcase & Portfolio

Your child completes their project with guidance on presenting it — whether that's publishing to an app store, deploying a website live, or sharing on GitHub. They walk away with something real to show.
