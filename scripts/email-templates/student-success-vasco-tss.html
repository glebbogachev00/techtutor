Student Success Story
A student-built game,
now on the App Store

Hello,

We wanted to share something we are genuinely proud of. One of our students, Vasco Connor , created and published a game called TSS - The Sustainable Squad .

It is an eco-adventure game that teaches real-world sustainability through play. The mission is simple and meaningful: help players think about the planet while giving them something interactive, fun, and useful to try.

What makes this special to us is that this is not just a class exercise. Vasco built a real product that people can download, play, and learn from.


Why this matters

When students build real projects, they learn more than software. They learn how to plan, improve, publish, and care about the people using what they make.


This is exactly the kind of confidence we want young learners to build: not just consuming technology, but creating something with purpose.
