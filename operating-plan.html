TechTutor is being optimized for quality, stability, and freedom — not growth.

A personal operating manual for running a small, high-quality, profitable education business that funds new products and gives the founders room to build.

North Star

Exceptional technology education through highly personalized learning experiences.

The goal is not to become the largest education company. The goal is to build a small, capable team that delivers outstanding outcomes for students and families.

Business Philosophy

We choose this, not that.

TechTutor stays lean
We prioritize
  • ✓Quality over quantity
  • ✓Long-term relationships over quick sales
  • ✓Premium service over mass-market offerings
  • ✓Small, capable teams over large organizations
  • ✓Product innovation over operational complexity
We deprioritize
  • ×Scaling for the sake of headcount
  • ×Cheap lessons for the largest audience
  • ×Roles that don't clearly add value
  • ×Operational complexity that distracts from teaching
  • ×Growth that lowers service quality
