FAQ

Why families choose TechTutor

Quick answers about our curriculum, tuition, schedules, and languages.

What does TechTutor teach? +

We cover Generative AI, coding (Scratch, Python, Lua), 3D design, and app/game development. Lessons mix creativity, storytelling, and technical foundations so students ship real projects.

Why choose TechTutor? +

Families join us for calm, project-first lessons, 3.5+ years of teaching expertise, and bilingual support. Every course includes feedback, session recaps, and portfolio-ready outcomes.

What's the tuition fee? +

Private lessons start from 5.6M VND for 8 sessions, and small-group lessons begin at 4M VND. Multi-course bundles unlock 5–10% savings. See our pricing section for full details.

How many lessons a week? +

Families typically book 2 sessions per week for steady progress. We can pace anywhere from 1–5 lessons weekly depending on goals, travel plans, and school workload.

Which languages do you teach in? +

Our instructors teach in English and Vietnamese. We frequently support bilingual households, and can switch languages mid-lesson if it helps your child feel confident.

How many lessons in one course? +

A single course bundle includes 8 lessons. It's enough to cover one project milestone or explore a new platform before deciding whether to extend.

How many lessons in a full program? +

Full programs span 24 lessons (three courses). This gives us time to move from foundations to advanced projects and deliver a polished portfolio piece.
